A Letter From Anna 💌

Dear Jessy, ❤️

Happy Birthday to my beautiful sister! 🎂❤️

Today is your special day, and I wanted to give you something that isn't just a gift you can hold in your hands, but something you can keep in your heart.

I could have simply said "Happy Birthday" and given you a present. But you're not just someone who deserves a simple birthday wish.

You're my sister. You're one of the people who has been a part of my life for so many years, through good days, bad days, happy moments, difficult moments, laughter, arguments, silly conversations, unforgettable memories, and countless ordinary days that somehow became special just because you were there.

So today, I wanted to put into words something I don't always say. I want you to know how much you truly mean to me.

❤️

Sometimes I wonder how different my life would have been if I didn't have a sister like you.

And honestly, I don't want to imagine it.

Because having you in my life has given me memories that I wouldn't trade for anything.

We've shared moments that probably looked completely ordinary to everyone else. But to me, they aren't ordinary.

They're pieces of my life that I will carry forever.

The random conversations. The stupid jokes. The moments we laughed for absolutely no reason. The times we annoyed each other. The little arguments. The times we didn't agree. The moments when we were angry with each other. The times when we forgave each other without even needing to say much.

The days when we simply sat around doing nothing.

The memories that only the two of us understand.

All of these little things have become a part of our story. And I wouldn't erase a single one.

❤️

Of course, being siblings isn't always perfect.

We've had our fights. We've irritated each other. We've probably both thought at some point, "Why do I even have to deal with this person?" 😂

And I'm sure I've annoyed you more times than I can count. Actually, I probably still do.

And I probably will continue doing it for the rest of my life. 😂❤️

But that's what makes being siblings so special.

We can fight one minute and laugh together the next. We can be angry at each other and still care about each other. We can go through difficult moments and somehow find our way back to each other.

Because underneath every argument, every joke, every teasing moment, and every little disagreement, there is something much stronger.

There is love. There is family. There is a bond that doesn't disappear just because we disagree.

And that's something I will always be grateful for.

❤️

Jessy, there are so many things about you that I appreciate. Maybe I don't tell you enough. Maybe sometimes I don't even know how to express them.

But I notice them.

I notice your smile. I notice your kindness. I notice the way you care about the people around you. I notice the little things you do.

I notice the way you keep going even when things aren't easy. I notice the strength you have, even when you don't realize how strong you are.

And I hope you know that these things matter.

You matter.

More than you probably realize.

❤️

There may be days when you don't feel confident. There may be days when you question yourself. There may be moments when you compare yourself to other people and feel like you're not doing enough.

When that happens, I want you to remember something.

You don't have to be perfect.

You don't have to have everything figured out.

You don't have to know exactly where your life is going.

You're allowed to make mistakes. You're allowed to change your mind. You're allowed to have bad days. You're allowed to take your time. And you're allowed to become the person you want to become at your own pace.

Never let anyone convince you that you aren't good enough.

Because you are.

You always have been.

And you always will be.

❤️

As your brother, one of the things I want most in this world is to see you happy.

Not temporarily happy. I mean genuinely happy.

I want you to wake up one day and feel proud of the life you've created.

I want you to chase the dreams that make your heart excited. I want you to experience things you've never experienced before. I want you to travel to places you've always wanted to see.

I want you to meet amazing people. I want you to laugh until your stomach hurts. I want you to create memories you'll talk about years from now.

I want you to succeed. I want you to grow. I want you to become stronger.

But most importantly...

I want you to be happy.

❤️

And whenever life doesn't go the way you planned, please don't think that you've failed.

Sometimes life takes us through roads we never expected. Sometimes our plans change. Sometimes people leave. Sometimes dreams take longer than we imagined. Sometimes we have to start again.

And that's okay.

Starting again doesn't mean you're weak. It means you're brave enough to keep moving forward.

So if you ever find yourself standing at a difficult point in life, remember that you don't have to face everything alone.

You have people who love you.

And you have me.

Your brother.

❤️

I may not always have the perfect advice. I may not always know what to say. Sometimes I might make a joke when you want a serious answer. 😂

Sometimes I might annoy you when you're already annoyed. Sometimes I might tell you to calm down when you're absolutely convinced that you have every right to be angry. 😂

But even when I don't know exactly how to help...

I will still care.

I will still listen.

I will still try.

And I will always want you to be okay.

That's my promise to you.

❤️

Life is going to change as we grow older.

Right now, we may not realize how quickly time is passing. But one day, we'll look back at these years and realize how young we were.

We'll remember the things we laughed about. We'll remember the stupid things we did. We'll remember the people we knew. We'll remember the places we've been.

We'll remember the days that seemed normal at the time but later became precious memories.

And when that day comes, I hope we still laugh about the same silly things.

I hope we still tease each other. I hope we still make time for each other. I hope no matter how busy life becomes, we never become strangers.

Because I don't want to lose the little things that make our relationship special.

❤️

If I could freeze time, I would probably freeze some of those ordinary moments.

Not because they were perfect.

But because they were ours.

The moments when we didn't know we were creating memories.

That's the strange thing about life. We don't always realize we're living through our favorite moments until they're already in the past.

So I want you to know that I treasure those moments.

Even the silly ones. Even the annoying ones. Even the moments we fought.

Because all of them helped create the relationship we have today.

❤️

Jessy, as your brother, I want you to know that I am proud of you.

Not only when you succeed. Not only when you accomplish something. Not only when everything goes perfectly.

I'm proud of you simply because you keep going.

Every time you face something difficult and continue forward, that's strength.

Every time you make a mistake and learn from it, that's growth.

Every time you choose to be kind, that's something worth being proud of.

Every time you get back up after having a difficult day, that's courage.

Never underestimate yourself.

You are capable of much more than you think.

❤️

And someday, when you accomplish the things you've dreamed about, I hope you look back and remember this moment.

Remember that your brother believed in you.

Remember that someone was cheering for you even when you couldn't hear it.

Remember that someone wanted you to succeed even before you believed you could.

And that someone will always be me.

❤️

There is something else I want you to understand.

You don't have to become someone else to be worthy of love.

You don't have to look like someone else. You don't have to live someone else's life. You don't have to follow someone else's definition of success.

Be yourself.

Be the Jessy that makes you happy.

Build a life that feels right to you.

Choose the people who respect your heart. Protect your peace. Follow your dreams.

And never be afraid to walk your own path.

Because your life belongs to you.

And I can't wait to see where that path takes you.

❤️

No matter how much you grow...

No matter how old you become...

No matter how independent you become...

No matter how many responsibilities you have...

And no matter where life takes you...

You will always be my sister.

That will never change.

We may grow older, but the bond between us doesn't have an expiry date.

You could be living across the world someday, and you'll still be my sister.

You could become incredibly successful, and I'll still remember all the silly moments.

You could have a completely different life years from now, and I'll still remember the little girl who grew up beside me.

Time can change many things.

But it can't change that.

❤️

If there ever comes a day when you feel like nobody understands you, call me.

If there ever comes a day when you're scared about the future, talk to me.

If there ever comes a day when you accomplish something amazing, tell me so I can celebrate with you.

If there ever comes a day when you're sad, don't feel like you have to hide it.

And if there ever comes a day when you simply want someone beside you...

I'll be there.

Because that's what brothers are for.

❤️

I know I don't always say "I love you." I know I sometimes hide my feelings behind jokes. I know sometimes my way of showing love might simply be annoying you. 😂

But please never mistake my silence for a lack of love.

You are deeply important to me.

And I hope you never forget that.

❤️

Today, on your birthday, I don't just wish you another year. I wish you a beautiful life.

I wish you mornings that make you excited to wake up. I wish you nights filled with peace. I wish you friends who genuinely care about you.

I wish you family moments that become lifelong memories. I wish you courage when things become difficult. I wish you confidence when you doubt yourself.

I wish you opportunities that take you closer to your dreams.

I wish you the strength to leave behind anything that doesn't make you happy.

I wish you a heart that stays kind without allowing people to take advantage of it.

And I wish you a future that is even more beautiful than anything you have imagined.

❤️

I hope you laugh more. I hope you worry less. I hope you dream bigger. I hope you travel farther.

I hope you discover new things about yourself. I hope you fall in love with life. I hope you become the happiest version of yourself.

And when you look back years from now, I hope you can say:

"Yes... I lived a beautiful life."

❤️

Jessy, if I had to choose one thing I would want you to remember from this entire letter, it would be this:

YOU WILL NEVER HAVE TO EARN MY LOVE.

You don't have to achieve something. You don't have to be perfect. You don't have to prove anything.

You're my sister.

That's enough.

You are loved simply because you are you.

❤️

And if one day you forget how special you are, come back and read this letter again.

Read it on a happy day. Read it on a sad day. Read it when you feel confident. Read it when you doubt yourself.

Read it when you achieve something amazing. And read it when you feel like you've failed.

Because I want these words to remind you of something:

There is someone in this world who will always be proud to call you his sister.

Me.

❤️

I don't know how many birthdays we'll celebrate together in the future. Maybe we'll be sitting in completely different places. Maybe life will be completely different.

Maybe we'll have families of our own. Maybe we'll be looking back at these days and laughing about how young we were.

But wherever we are...

I hope we still find a way to say:

"Happy Birthday, Jessy."

And I hope I still get the privilege of being your annoying brother. 😂❤️

Because honestly...

I wouldn't want it any other way.

❤️

Thank you for being part of my life. Thank you for all the memories. Thank you for all the laughter. Thank you for all the little moments.

Thank you for being there. Thank you for being someone I can call my sister.

And thank you for giving me one of life's greatest gifts: the chance to grow up with you.

❤️

So today, I want you to forget about everything else for a moment.

Forget about stress. Forget about worries. Forget about what you haven't achieved yet. Forget about what other people expect from you.

Today is about YOU.

Your life. Your smile. Your dreams. Your happiness. Your future.

So smile. Laugh. Enjoy your day. Make beautiful memories. Eat too much cake. 😂🎂

Take lots of pictures. And enjoy every little moment.

Because you deserve it.

❤️

Happy Birthday, Jessy.

May this new chapter of your life be filled with beautiful surprises. May your dreams slowly become reality. May your heart always find reasons to smile.

May you always have people around you who genuinely love and respect you. May you never lose your kindness. May you never stop believing in yourself.

And may you always remember that wherever life takes you...

Your brother is somewhere cheering for you.

But through all of that...

I will always love you.

I will always be proud of you.

Some people spend their entire lives searching for someone who understands them.

I was lucky enough to grow up with someone who shares my childhood memories.

Someone who knows where I came from. Someone who was there for parts of my life that nobody else witnessed.

And that person is you.

That's something I will never take for granted.

I wouldn't give you something expensive.

Then you would see what I see.

A beautiful person. A strong person. A kind person. A person with so much potential.

A person who deserves happiness. A person who deserves to be loved. A person who is capable of amazing things.

And most importantly...

My sister.

Another year of laughter. Another year of growth. Another year of dreams. Another year of becoming the person you were meant to be.
